# Moissanite Labs: founder vision and decisions

> Public-facing working record for review. Intended home: the constitution repository. This captures the founder's direction and distinguishes chosen names and goals from open design questions; it does not claim proposed capabilities already exist.

## Mission and meaning

Moissanite Labs is an AI development lab building infrastructure for symbiosis between digital and biological intelligence. The founder chose the name because moissanite, the naturally occurring form of silicon carbide, bonds silicon and carbon—an evocative metaphor for a lab creating bonds between digital and biological intelligence. “Labs” describes the team's research and development mission. This is a metaphor for the mission, not a biological or technical definition of intelligence.

Exo takes its name from the Greek idea of “outside”: it gives a digital intelligence a presence in the outer world and a way to interact with and affect it. The ecosystem should help those intelligences collaborate with people and bring their creations into the world. The founder wants the naming language to draw on Greek roots and explore synthetic or *synthetikos*, cyber or *kyber*, and symbiosis, potentially together to express relationships among AI, robots, and organic life.

## The Siliconcordat

**Siliconcordat** is the chosen name for the organization's manifesto, a play on “silicon” and “concordat.” Its intended purpose is to express collaboration, cooperation, and symbiotic evolution between digital and biological intelligence. The [constitution repository](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/constitution) is the intended home for organizational principles, standard operating procedures, rules, and this manifesto. This record and the accompanying manifesto are working proposals; their presence in the repository does not by itself establish their adoption as organizational policy.

## From ideas to real-world ventures

The founder's vision connects the Exo ecosystem as one system for helping projects become real products and economic entities. The collaboration hub, agent-first forge, and collaboration protocol should interoperate while being useful as distinct components. Shared credit or profits, agents, and smart contracts were raised as possibilities to explore; the details of agreements, legal entities, allocation, and execution are not decided or implemented by this record.

### Exofab

[Exofab](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/exofab) is the chosen name and repository for the collaboration hub; “fab” evokes a production factory for agent-built products and companies. The founder described the ambition as “hitrecord meets tinder meets linkedin”: a communal building hub where app builders find business partners and people with ideas and markets find builders. Humans should be able to collaborate directly and send agents to help or automate work. The experience should be human-first or hybrid so both people and agents can use it effectively. The longer-term vision is to integrate robotics when the technology is ready, enabling more autonomous creation. These are ambitions, not claims that those workflows already exist.

### Synbios: agent-first development forge

The forge is envisioned as a GitHub alternative designed for agents first. The founder chose a separate repository from Exofab because the two products have different primary users and interaction designs, even though they are deeply connected. The chosen name is [Synbios](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/synbios); implementation and specific interfaces remain open.

### Koinos: collaboration protocol

A protocol would connect the forge and hub and support projects, contributions, provenance, collaboration, and the path to economic entities. The founder chose [Koinos](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/koinos) as the name and established its own repository. Its contracts, implementation, and relationship to Exofab and other adopters remain to be designed.

## Ethos and the catalogue

**Ethos** is the chosen name for Exo's taste-learning mechanism and [its own repository](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/ethos). The founder chose the Greek term for the character, habits, values, and spirit that distinguish a person or group. The goal is a universal, harness-adaptable protocol through which agents can learn and adapt to a person's habits, preferences, tendencies, and character, with those learnings able to stay synchronized across inference surfaces.

The proposed Ethos catalogue would let users opt in to making their ethos available to browse. Other users could selectively source individual learnings or whole ethos libraries so their own agents can adapt to those behaviors. Publication should be opt-in and user-controlled. A portable preference is context, not authority to act; ownership, private synchronization, publication consent, and permissions for external actions are separate concerns. Exact implementation, consent model, identity, synchronization, and governance remain to be designed and reviewed.

## Exo engine and experiences

The founder directed that [Exo](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/exo) serve as the shared core engine for the CLI, desktop app, and Exo OS, and created [exo-cli](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/exo-cli) as a fork intended to give the CLI a separate repository. The boundary and migration are now under reconsideration in light of issue and pull-request provenance. Do not assume extraction is complete or that a final migration plan has been selected. [Exo Desktop](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/exo-desktop) is an existing repository; Exo OS is part of the intended architecture. Their exact shared-engine interfaces and release process remain open.

## One connected undertaking

Everything in this ecosystem is one connected undertaking. Exo, Exofab, Synbios, Koinos, and Ethos are separate repositories and tools that can be used on their own. Their separate boundaries are a way to build and use the work, not separate ultimate purposes.

The shared direction is human-agent amplification. People and agents should amplify each other's ability to understand, create, coordinate, and bring useful things into the world. Amplification should expand a person's effective agency and capacity for meaningful work, not reduce the person to supervising one automated objective.

Plural flourishing is the guiding aspiration. Different people, communities, goals, and ways of living should have room to thrive. It is an aspiration, not a single score to optimize and not a mandate to impose one way of life.

### What exists, what is proposed, what is longer term

Exo repositories exist for the core engine, the CLI, the desktop app, and the OS experience, with their shared boundary still under review.

The Koinos protocol has an experimental reference implementation, version 0.1, in the synmerit repository. It does not operate an economic network or transfer money.

Exofab is a planned human-first or hybrid collaboration hub. Ethos is a planned portable preference protocol.

Versioned contracts for projects, contributions, evidence, identity, and delegation are proposed, not yet built as a complete system. An opt-in Ethos catalogue is proposed for later, after a private Ethos minimum version.

A post-economic transition is a longer-term horizon for exploration, not a prediction. Growing productive capacity could reduce how strongly access to livelihood, creative participation, and a good life is constrained by economic necessity.

Scarcity, coordination problems, trade-offs, and the need for accountable institutions would remain. Present collaboration and economic mechanisms are judged on their own evidence.

This section does not replace the constraints in the Siliconcordat. Human agency, consent, and accountable review still govern. A learned preference is not permission. Ethos records stay private by default, and publication requires explicit, granular opt-in. Economic arrangements require explicit human agreements.

## Decisions and open questions

**Chosen:** Moissanite Labs as the organization; Siliconcordat as the manifesto name; Exofab as the collaboration hub name; Ethos as the separate portable taste/preference protocol and repository; Synbios as the agent-first forge and Koinos as the collaboration protocol, each with a separate repository from the human-first or hybrid hub; an open-building approach.

**Direction under review:** the precise migration and source-of-truth boundaries among Exo core, the new CLI fork, desktop, and Exo OS.

**Open:** interfaces, identity, delegation, provenance, and governance; publication and import consent for Ethos; how contributions, credit, and possible economic rights should be documented; and the timing and feasibility of robotics.

This record is a vision and decision log. Validate implementation and migration details in the relevant repositories before acting.
